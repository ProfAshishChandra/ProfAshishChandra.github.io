About

Optimization for decisions under uncertainty

My research lies at the intersection of operations research, mathematical optimization, applied probability, and network systems. I am particularly interested in certifying and improving the performance of systems exposed to failures, demand uncertainty, and other operational disruptions.

Research interests

Network and system reliability, robust network design, transportation and supply chains, uncertainty quantification, financial-network contagion, and kidney exchange.

Methodological interests

Mathematical programming, stochastic optimization, decision making under uncertainty, algorithm design, applied probability, and statistics.

Research

Selected research themes

01

Reliable & Resilient Networks

Optimization-based methods to measure, certify, and improve the reliability of networked systems under random or adversarial disruptions.

02

Supply Chain Risk & Robustness

Robust and stochastic models for supply-chain design and operations under uncertain failures, demand, service requirements, and sustainability objectives.

03

Risk-Averse Optimization

Mathematical methods for probability estimation, risk measures, uncertainty quantification, and decision making under uncertainty.
