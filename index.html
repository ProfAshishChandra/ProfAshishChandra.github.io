I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Management and Quantitative Methods at Illinois State University. My research focuses on optimization and decision making under uncertainty, with applications to network reliability, resilient supply chains, transportation systems, and risk-aware decision making.

I received my Ph.D. in Management with a specialization in Quantitative Methods from Purdue University, with a minor in Electrical and Computer Engineering. I received my M.S. in Operations Research from the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay and my B.S. in Mathematics from the University of Mumbai's DAE Centre for Excellence in Basic Sciences.

Research

My research interests include network and system reliability, robust network design, transportation and supply-chain problems, uncertainty quantification, contagion in financial networks, and kidney exchange.

Methodologically, I work in mathematical programming, stochastic optimization, decision making under uncertainty, algorithm design, applied probability, and statistics.

I have served as a session chair at INFORMS, IISE, DSI, and MidWest DSI meetings, including sessions on supply-chain resilience, supply-chain management, risk mitigation, machine learning and optimization, and nonlinear optimization.

My reviewing activities include Mathematical Programming Computation, the Academy of Management Annual Conference, IISE Annual Conference, and Mid-West Decision Science Institute.
