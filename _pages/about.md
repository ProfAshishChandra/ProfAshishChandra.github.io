I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Management and Quantitative Methods, College of Business, Illinois State University. My research develops optimization and probabilistic methods for decision-making under uncertainty, with applications to resilient supply chains, networked systems, healthcare and pharmaceutical supply chains, and risk-aware operations.

## Research Interests

**Applications:** Network/System Reliability and Resilience; Robust Network Design; Transportation and Mobility Systems; Supply Chain Network Optimization; Healthcare and Pharmaceutical Supply Chains; Risk Modeling; Uncertainty Quantification; Financial Networks and Systemic Risk.

**Methodologies:** Mathematical Programming; Robust and Stochastic Optimization; Chance-Constrained Optimization; Integer and Nonlinear Optimization; Decomposition and Relaxation Methods; Dynamic Programming; Applied Probability; Statistics; Prescriptive Analytics.

## Teaching Interests

Operations Management; Management Science; Business Decision Making and Decision Analysis; Business Analytics and Quantitative Analysis; Applied Probability and Statistics; Deterministic and Stochastic Operations Research; Mathematical Programming; Convex Optimization; Optimization under Uncertainty; Supply Chain and Service Operations.

## Research Areas

### Robust and Stochastic Optimization

This research develops mathematical models and computational methods for decision-making under uncertainty, including robust and adjustable optimization, stochastic optimization, risk measures, integer and nonlinear optimization, decomposition, relaxation, and tractable approximations.

[More on this area →](/research/#robust-and-stochastic-optimization)

### Supply Chain Network Resilience

This research studies the design, operation, and validation of supply chain networks exposed to disruptions, uncertain demand, partial and multi-state failures, geopolitical shocks, and other sources of operational uncertainty.

[More on this area →](/research/#supply-chain-network-resilience)

### Risk, Reliability, and Performance Analytics

This research develops quantitative methods for performance certification, disruption-scenario analysis, exact counting, tail-risk modeling, and reliability assessment in networked systems.

[More on this area →](/research/#risk-reliability-and-performance-analytics)
