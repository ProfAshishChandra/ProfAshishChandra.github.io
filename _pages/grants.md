## Research Grants and Sponsored Projects

### PharmaShield: A Risk-Aware System for Resilient & Equitable Pharmacy Supply

**Connected Communities Initiative (CCI) — Spring 2026 Award**  
**Funding partnership:** Illinois State University and OSF HealthCare  
**Role:** Principal Investigator  
**Award:** $74,909.80  
**Project period:** July 2026–June 2027

Leading an interdisciplinary collaboration with pharmacy leadership, clinicians, and operational stakeholders at OSF HealthCare in Peoria to develop and pilot a data-driven, risk-aware decision-support framework for resilient pharmacy supply operations. The project integrates demand and supply-risk analytics, stochastic optimization, 340B-sensitive prioritization, and clinician-informed validation to translate quantitative models into implementable inventory, allocation, and redistribution strategies for improving medication access and system resilience.

[Illinois State University project announcement](https://news.illinoisstate.edu/2026/05/osf-healthcare-and-illinois-state-university-announce-spring-2026-connected-communities-initiative-awardees/)

## Teaching and Instructional Grants

### Instructional Innovation Grant

**Illinois State University**  
**Role:** Principal Investigator  
**Award:** $500  
**Period:** 2025–2026

**Project:** “Immersive Simulations for Inclusive Decision-Making: Bridging Theory and Practice Through Data-Driven Case Studies.”

Supported the design and implementation of immersive, data-driven business simulations that engage students in applying quantitative concepts to realistic decision problems. The simulations integrate structured analysis, iterative decision making, and reflection to strengthen students’ ability to translate theory into practice while promoting active and inclusive participation in operations and analytics courses.

### Scholarship of Teaching and Learning (SoTL) Travel Grant

**Illinois State University**  
**Role:** Principal Investigator  
**Award:** $750  
**Period:** 2025–2026

**Project:** “Immersive Simulations for Inclusive Decision-Making: A Statistical Evaluation of Equity-Centered Pedagogy.”

Supported the dissemination of an empirical study evaluating how immersive, data-driven simulations influence student learning, engagement, and equity outcomes in quantitative business courses. The research examines conceptual learning gains, applied decision-making performance, and differences in learning outcomes across student groups, and was presented at the 2026 Midwest Decision Sciences Institute Annual Conference.
